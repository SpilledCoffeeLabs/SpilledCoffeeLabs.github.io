Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to personal data when you play Dunefall, the mobile game (Android and iOS), and when you visit this website. Dunefall has no user accounts, and its game data stays on your device. Personal data is processed only for advertising (with your consent where the law requires it) and for in-app purchases.

2. Data stored only on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, best scores), settings (sound, language, rider and horse), items, tries, the daily gift and a cached copy of the passes you bought are stored locally on your device. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. The game also has optional test tools that can record games and frame times on the device; they are off by default and their data never leaves the device.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Dunefall is free to play and shows ads: full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded ads (for example, to continue a level). The ads are delivered by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. AdMob may also pass ad requests to other advertising networks ("mediation partners"); they are listed in the consent form.

Data processed: the device's advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system information, app information, approximate location (derived from the IP address), and how you interact with ads (views, clicks), plus diagnostic data about ad delivery.

Purposes: showing ads, limiting how often you see them, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud, and, if you agree, showing ads based on your interests (personalised ads).

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021) for storing and reading information on your device and for personalised ads. Without consent, ads may still be shown on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, financing the free game) in a limited, non-personalised form where the law allows it.

Your choice: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before it requests ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy settings in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Google may process data outside the EU, including in the USA; Google is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. More information: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

With the Deputy Pass or Sheriff Pass, the game shows no full-screen ads; rewarded ads only appear when you choose to watch one.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play, Apple App Store, RevenueCat)

Purchases are made and paid through the app store (Google Play by Google Ireland Limited, or the Apple App Store by Apple Distribution International Ltd.). We never see your payment details. The store's own privacy policy applies to the payment.

To check purchases, deliver what you bought and restore passes on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat, a service of RevenueCat, Inc., 1032 E Brandon Blvd #3003, Brandon, FL 33511, USA.

Data processed: a random app user ID created by RevenueCat (not your name or email), the products bought, prices and currency, purchase time, the store's purchase receipt or token, country, device model, operating system and app version, and the IP address needed to connect.

Purpose and legal basis: carrying out the purchase contract and restoring purchases (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and keeping records required by tax law (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

RevenueCat processes data in the USA. The transfer is based on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and, where RevenueCat is certified, on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. More information: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

6. Contact by email

If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them when they are no longer needed, unless the law requires us to keep them.

7. What we do not do

Dunefall has no user accounts, no analytics or crash-reporting service of our own, and no social login. We do not sell personal data.

8. How long data is kept

Data on your device: until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Advertising data: as set out in Google's and the partners' policies. Purchase records: as long as needed to provide and restore your purchases, and as long as tax law requires (in Austria, generally seven years).

9. Children

Dunefall is not directed at children under 13. Where a player is below the age at which they can consent to online services in their country (in Austria 14, in some EU countries up to 16), consent must be given by a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future. Because we do not hold game data or identifiers on our own servers, requests about advertising or purchase data are best directed to Google or RevenueCat as well; we will help you with this.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Austria this is the Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at.

11. Changes

We will update this policy when the game or the law changes. The date above shows the current version.
